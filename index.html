01

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Device

By default, the App stores the content you create locally on your device. This includes:

  • Journal entries and textual content
  • Mood logs and associated tags / metadata
  • User preferences and in-app configuration (theme, reminder time, etc.)

Device & Diagnostic Data

We may collect non-identifiable technical data to help diagnose crashes and improve the App. This data may include device model, OS version, and anonymous usage metrics. We will not collect personal journal content as part of diagnostics unless you explicitly opt in to a crash report that includes user content.

Third-Party Services

If you choose to enable optional features that integrate with third parties (for example cloud sync, backups, analytics, or advertising), those services may collect or process data according to their own policies. We will present an explicit opt-in flow before enabling any such service.

02

How We Use Information

  • To provide core App functionality (save entries, show reminders, track moods)
  • To deliver notifications and schedule reminders
  • To analyze anonymous usage patterns to improve features and performance (only if you opt in)
  • To respond to user support requests and resolve issues
03

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share your personal journal entries or mood logs with third parties. The only circumstances where information might be shared are:

  • With your explicit consent (for example when you enable cloud backup)
  • To comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or government request
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public

Advertising IDs

We may use advertising IDs to display ads and measure ad performance via third-party ad services (for example, Google AdMob). These IDs are used only for advertising purposes and are not linked to your personal journal content.

04

Storage, Retention & Security

✅

Local storage by default. All journal data is stored on your device. We have no access to your personal entries.

Backups & Cloud Sync

If you opt into cloud backup or synchronization, your data will be transmitted to the selected provider and retained according to that provider's policies. We do not access or view your personal entries when you use a third-party backup provider.

Security Measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data under our control. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We recommend enabling device-level security (PIN, passcode, biometric) and using Journeo's built-in App Lock feature to protect access.

05

Children

⚠️

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove the information promptly.

06

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & portability: Because data is primarily stored locally, you can export your entries using the App's export feature. Contact us for assistance.
  • Deletion: You may delete your journal entries within the App, or uninstall the App to remove locally stored data. If you have enabled cloud backups, you must also delete data from the backup provider.
  • Opt-out of analytics: Analytics and diagnostic collection are disabled by default. You may enable or disable them in App settings where applicable.
07

Third-Party Links & Services

The App may include links to third-party websites, services, or SDKs. These third parties have their own privacy policies; we encourage you to review them before providing any data.

08

International Transfers

If you enable cloud features that transmit data to servers outside your country, your data may be transferred across international borders. Where applicable, we will seek to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here with a revised effective date. It is your responsibility to review this policy periodically.
